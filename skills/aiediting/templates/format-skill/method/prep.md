## Preparation before a shoot

When the person is about to record, `python "<skillDir>/tools/run.py" prep "<topic>"` writes `подготовка.md` into the issue folder: the empty map, the camera, the checklist. Fill the map **with them**, from their topic and in their words: 7–12 steps, each with a name of 2–5 words and 3–5 points to say. Nothing is made up about their life. If they read a text, write it in their voice: short phrases, spoken words, no officialese, blocks of 1–2 minutes.

After the shoot the file goes beside the source. `run.py edit` then lists the map's steps, and they are the chapters: when you answer the `chapters` question, take each title from the map and put its start at the phrase where the speaker begins that step.

Asked what to shoot ahead for a library: ten or twenty short horizontal clips of 5–10 s, steady, from the subject of their blog (hands at work, places, a routine, details), each a thing a speaker may mention; they go into the library's `футажи/` and are described like the rest.
