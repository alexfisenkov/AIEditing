## The person's library

One folder on the person's own disk, found by `python "<skillDir>/tools/run.py" library where`; every `монтаж-*` of theirs finds the same one. It answers «нет»: nothing here applies, and you do not ask them to connect it in the middle of an edit. Everything stays on their disk and nothing in it is copied into this skill. Rules and the list of what not to show are in it too: see «How to take remarks».

**Describing files, a little at a time.** `run.py edit` tells you when new files appeared in the folders the library already knows. For each kind (`галерея`, `instagram`, `футажи`, `стоки`): `run.py library sheet <kind>` makes a numbered sheet of small frames and lists the files; look at the sheet, not at each file, and write one row per file into a JSON list: `f`, `date`, `where`, `who`, `what` (one line), `type` (`фото` or `видео`), `orient`, `dur`, `best` (`[[from, to]]` seconds), `quality`, `tags`, `phrases` (what a speaker would say when it fits), `never`, and for footage `plan`, `motion`, `log`. Then `run.py library describe <kind> <file.json>`. The progress is saved by every batch: when a limit ends, the next run goes on from the same place, and what is described is never described twice. Work in batches in the background and keep the answers short.

**When you are not sure** who or what is in a frame, ask them: «Я правильно понимаю, что это …?» with two or three frames, a yes or a no. To find an important person on every sheet, ask for two or three of their photos and look for them on the sheets. Names live only in the library.

**Getting to know them, 3–5 questions at a time**, in plain talk, not a form: where they come from and when; work by years; where they lived; sports and hobbies by years; who the people close to them are and what they look like in photos; turning points; the blog; where and how they shoot. Each answer goes at once into the files of `обо-мне/` (`кто-я.md`, `путь-жизни.md`, `люди.md`, `места.md`, `хобби.md`, `блог.md`) in their words, short, as anchors: «2019 — работал в магазине кроссовок, Москва». An answer that contradicts what is written is asked about, never written over. Documents (a work record, diplomas) become a short extract in `обо-мне/документы/`; a passport or other identity papers are never taken. About a fifth of the gallery told by them is enough: you tie the rest yourself.

**Where the files come from.** Gallery: on a Mac with iCloud photos, Photos → Settings → iCloud → download originals, then export the unmodified originals into a folder; on Windows the iCloud app or the phone's DCIM folder; on Android Google Takeout. Work on that copy, never inside the Photos database. Instagram: Accounts Center → your information and permissions → download your information → the profile → all available information (messages only if they want them) → to the device → all time → JSON → high quality; the mail with a link comes in a day or more, up to a few days or longer; download every part into `instagram/` and `run.py library scan instagram <folder>`. Telegram is optional, by its own export. Footage from a camera: described by topic with the plan (wide, medium, close), the movement of the camera, the best stretches, `log` for a log profile and `never` for what must not be used; bad takes are marked, not deleted.

**Albums** are made only after you show a test structure (the list and five examples each) and they say «ок»; by links, never by moving originals. A person who says «такого-то не бери» goes to `run.py forbid "<who or what>"` at once.
