## The timeline file

Where the pipeline has `timeline`, the issue folder holds `таймлиния/<name>.fcpxml` (FCPXML 1.10) and the note `таймлиния.md`. DaVinci Resolve imports it: File → Import → Timeline, and «да» when it asks about the media. Say that in your last message in two lines, and what the note says about the speed to set. No program is driven from outside: a remark means a rebuilt file, imported again, and the old timeline stays until they say «ок».

Where the format has graphics over the video, the picture is one clip and the sound is in separate stems (voice, sounds, music variants) by `overlay.py timeline <working folder> <issue folder>`. Elsewhere the layers: V1 the camera pieces (with several cameras the angles), the inserts above the plates, the plates, then the voice, the music and the sounds on their own lines. Markers stand on chapters, inserts and edits: the person checks the markers, not the whole video. A new version of a file is a new name; never write over a file the program already knows.

Nobody has tried the import unless it was tried: say so. A person with no editing program, and with no wish for one, gets the finished video and gives remarks by timecode: «на 03:12 убери вставку».

The little they need in DaVinci: import the file rather than dragging media in; work on the Edit page; space plays and pauses; J, K, L go back, stop and forward; the square at the left of a line switches it on and off (the variants of music are chosen that way); M puts a marker.
