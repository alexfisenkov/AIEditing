# What's new in AIEditing

## 0.4.1 — 2026-11-14

For a person who sets nothing new the workshop and the format skills work as in 0.4.0.

Added:
- The workshop tells you when a new version is out. At its start it compares its version with the latest release on GitHub, at most once a day and in a few seconds, and stays silent without the network. A newer one is one line: which version came out, which one you have, the exact way to update your copy (`npx skills update aiediting -g`, or `git pull` for a clone) and that a new session is needed. It updates only on your word, never in the middle of a session. `AIEDITING_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1` turns it off.
- A format skill built on this version says the same at `run.py check` and `run.py edit` when the workshop on the computer, or the latest release, is newer than the one it was built on: start AIEditing by its command (/aiediting in Claude Code, $aiediting in Codex) and choose a refinement of that skill to rebuild it. The edit goes on, nothing is installed.
- What you tell at the start is kept (`ae.py intro`) and used to the end: the formats are proposed from your answer, your topic and the videos you like and dislike go into the format's taste by themselves, and with a library your blog, your equipment and what must never be shown go into its files. Formats, the interview and the design no longer ask what you already said.
- `ae.py profile check` and `build-skill` warn of a choice the disk does not back: inserts without a library or with nothing described, a music folder that is not there or holds no track, a voice whose key is not in its variable, cameras no trial has lined up, a sound folder that is not there.
- Refining a skill offers what is new since it was built as a short list to pick from.
- Codex no longer starts the workshop by itself (`agents/openai.yaml`); the installation and the calls of Gemini CLI and OpenClaw are described, and so is `/skill монтаж-<слово>` in OpenClaw, which makes commands of Latin names only.

Fixed:
- The environment is checked before the personal plan, and the plan's first step is the choice of a new skill or a refinement.
- The tool is looked for in the folder of the skill your agent loaded, then in this project, then in the global folders of every agent; two copies of different versions are named.
- The description of your files in the library begins after the «да» to the first trial, in one place; stock videos are said to need a library, as they always did.
- The pace of Reels and Shorts goes only into formats that make vertical or short clips, not into a lesson or a stream.
- A trial with several cameras is run with all of them; one camera is one rule everywhere; DaVinci is offered once, in the interview.
- What a format skill does and when to call it come first in its description, so an agent with many skills still sees it.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-13

A refinement, not a remake: the formats, phases and profiles that exist work as they did. Everything new is a choice of the person; what they do not have or do not want is not set up, not inserted and not made up.

- Getting to know the person: the workshop starts with one message (computer and disk, videos, cameras and sound, an editing program, topic and audience, material of their own, services they have, videos they like and dislike, what must never be shown) and a personal plan. What it already knows it does not ask again. Whether to use their own shot material and connect a library is asked slowly, with what they get and that «no» breaks nothing; the first trial never waits for it.
- The library: one folder of the person's own on their disk, found by every format skill of theirs (`ae.py library`, `run.py library`); files about them, a numbered-sheet inventory of the gallery, an Instagram export, camera footage and stock; their rules and the list of what never to show.
- Inserts (`broll`) from the library under the literal meaning of a phrase, with free stock by the person's own key and a re-shoot list; the rules of the joints and lengths; a person they ask not to show is written into the list and never shown again.
- A timeline for DaVinci Resolve (`timeline`, FCPXML) beside the video, in layers or as one picture with separate sound stems; no program, no timeline: the video and remarks by timecode.
- Voice through Auphonic on the person's key, with the preset chosen by ear (`ae.py voice-try`); music from the person's folder or a service they have, in variants and by chapter; sounds by role on the peak of the motion of graphics (`overlay.py peaks`), a catalog for the person's own sound folder (`overlay.py sfxcat`).
- A publishing pack (`publish_pack`): three titles, a YouTube description with chapters by YouTube's rules, tags, frames for covers; six covers on request (`run.py covers`). Nothing is uploaded or published.
- Several cameras and a sound recorded apart (`angles`); gentle cleaning of speech next to the dense one (`params.cleanup`, `params.repeats`); joints without clicks everywhere.
- Preparation before a shoot (`run.py prep "<topic>"`): a map of 7–12 steps, the camera, a checklist; a map beside the source gives the chapters.
- Every remark of the person is written into their own rules at once (`run.py note`, `run.py forbid`) and followed from the next video; the steps and design of a format still change only through «AIEditing — доработка».
- Interview: a row for each new key of the profile, and «chosen means proved»: a key that answers, a folder that holds tracks, a program that opens, before the skill is built. «Without questions» uses what is set up and names what is not.
- Format skills get a method for each chosen step (`library`, `broll`, `sound`, `publish`, `timeline`, `angles`, `prep`) and one on how to give remarks; the overlay kit gets `recipes.md` (show, do not write: three levels, alternating formats, the camera window, no two identical screens, events on the word, exits beyond the edge) and rules of pace and captions for vertical clips.

## 0.3.3 — 2026-11-13

- Overlay kit: the face check of a render. Right after every change of the picture (0.2 s and 1 s after every cut the face track knows, every joint of an edit by markers across which the face jumps, and in a vertical clip every joint of two spans) the speaker's face must be whole inside the speaker's window. The frames are rendered from the composition itself, as the render is (`tools/face-frames.mjs`); the presenter reports where its window is (`src/kit/speakerBox.ts`, only when asked); YuNet looks inside it. Findings: «НЕТ ЛИЦА В РАМКЕ», «ЛИЦО СРЕЗАНО КРАЕМ РАМКИ», «РАМКА СПИКЕРА НЕ СООБЩЕНА», each with the second and the frame's file.
- A face cut by the edge is told by where it ends: the detector sees only what the window shows, so the face it finds ends on the edge, while a whole face keeps a margin of forehead, cheeks and chin (`tools/overlaylib/facejudge.py`).
- Where it runs: `overlay.py face <W> --check`; `draft` after the render (exit 1); `final` and `vertical-final` before the render, stopping unless `--face-accepted "reason"`; `vertical-check` for every clip, into its `findings.txt` too. Unchecked is never a pass: no OpenCV or model, no face track, a track or a presenter of the earlier kind make `face --check` stop with the reason, `draft` fail and `final` stop. `final --reuse-render` is refused when the render is older than the scenes, layouts, kit or track, because the check looks at the composition.
- A folder made by 0.3.2 or earlier: `face`, `prepare`, `vertical-init` and the check itself name a presenter that does not report its window, with what to do. Every render command adds the kit's new modules (`faceTrack.ts`, `speakerBox.ts`) to such a folder when they are missing, and replaces nothing.
- Tried on 12 seconds of a real recording around a switch from the screen to the camera, every step logged: a folder of 0.3.2 is refused with its reason, `final` included; after the upgrade the tools name, the track by pieces passes; the values of a track smoothed through the switch, as before 0.3.2, give two findings, 0.2 s and 1 s after it, and `final` stops. A vertical clip across the switch: passes with the track by pieces, two findings with the smoothed one, `vertical-final` stops. All eight layouts of the long video, a frame every 0.5 s and in the middle of the changes of layout: no false finding in 24 frames.
- Not looked at: frames between two changes of the picture; the speaker's circle of a vertical call to action (a second layer); a joint of an edit by markers where the face stays in place (there may be hundreds, and the track by pieces holds them by itself); the finished file itself. A scene that covers the speaker on purpose over a cut, and a speaker turned away, are findings for the eye and `--face-accepted`. Skills `монтаж-*` installed earlier carry their own copy of the kit and get the check when they are rebuilt.

## 0.3.2 — 2026-11-13

- Overlay kit: the speaker's frame no longer lags behind the face after a hard cut or a scene change. Before this the face track was smoothed through the whole video, twice (`prepare`, then the presenter), so after a switch from a screen recording to the camera the frame glided to the new face for seconds and cut it. Now `prepare` finds where the face jumps between two adjacent frames, writes the second each point's piece began as the fifth number of a point in `face.json`, and smooths inside a piece only. `src/kit/faceTrack.ts` reads the track for the long video and for vertical clips alike: median, Gaussian and spline stay inside one piece, and the edges of the time map's rows and of a clip's spans are cuts too. A head that only moves fast is not a cut, and neither is a frame the detector lost in the blur of such a move. Inside one piece the frame glides as before.
- A vertical clip counts the second of a frame from the very frame at which the video layer starts a span (`src/vertical/spanTime.ts`). Before this the two disagreed for one frame at about every other joint of two spans: one frame of the new picture in the frame and layout of the old one.
- `overlay.py face <W>` watches the track of a working folder: it lists every cut, names a track or a presenter of the earlier kind, rebuilds the track without losing the earlier one (`--rebuild`; in a folder edited by markers only from the first accepted video, `--video`), and renders a still 0.2 s after every cut of the track and every joint of an edit by markers across which the face jumps (`--frames`). `prepare` and `vertical-init` warn about the same. `prepare` no longer advises deleting `face.json`.
- Tried on one real recording of 31 minutes, camera and screen in one file: all six switches between the camera and the screen were found within a frame of where the scenes had them, and one cut between takes. 0.2 s after a switch the frame is centred on the new face; the kit before this was 0.18 of the frame width off there and showed the edge of the picture in the speaker's circle.
- A face on a shared screen that is clearly larger than the speaker's circle still takes the frame, and now at once instead of slowly. Working folders and installed `монтаж-*` skills made earlier keep their own copy of the kit and change only when they are renewed or rebuilt.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-11-10

- The parent now carries task-specific tool methods: scenes, audio measurements/sync/processing, subtitles, colour/stabilization, background/alpha, vision/OCR/transcription, graphics, editorial choices and technical QA. Each method names source-time ownership, parameter choices, failure signs and acceptance checks.
- `tools/recipes.py` provides checked, local scene/audio reports, recorder-offset analysis, subtitle conversion/burn-in, one-cut stabilization and aligned same-content VMAF. It selects the private tool environment and protects sources. Validated results use atomic no-clobber placement where available, or an exclusive copy on other volumes; command success is the completion receipt. These examples do not add native pipeline steps or change a format profile.
- Supplied motion/editing skills are adapted to the existing runtime and linked to a source/coverage ledger; unavailable editor APIs and personal assets are not bundled. Four installed montage formats are outside this update's write scope.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-10

- `ae.py setup` prepares the complete local editing toolkit; `setup --check` verifies it without installing, and selected steps can repair their own dependencies. Failed installs and unusable binaries now fail the command. Checks cover package versions, model hashes, ffprobe, renderer CLIs and renderer browsers.
- The agent treats an editing request as permission to prepare its local tools. A missing catalog preset leads to a real tool choice and a tested editing method, rather than a categorical refusal. `capabilities --json` reports actual tool and FFmpeg-filter readiness across editing families; it does not certify the quality of an unrendered video.
- RVM ONNX performs local human-video background removal to transparent ProRes 4444 MOV, replacement with a colour/image/video or blur, and foreground placement over graphics. Chroma key uses FFmpeg. Source files stay intact, output is staged atomically, audio and duration are checked. Image/object cutout uses the explicitly selected BiRefNet Lite model; no implicit default model is downloaded.
- Additional local tools cover shot detection, audio analysis and subtitle formats. Dependencies and render lockfiles are updated against current stable upstream releases, with compatible Python pins and a separate managed interpreter when the host is too old. Windows whisper.cpp uses a checksum-verified official build from the stable source commit.
- Generated format skills carry a small standalone toolkit installer and selected dependency locks. Their setup can repair the chosen tools even after the parent skill is removed. Existing installed formats are not silently rebuilt.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-05

- `params.speed` goes on once, in `run.py edit`. The clean speech stays at the speed of the recording, so its `speed` no longer doubles (1.145 instead of 1.07), and a recogniser hears normal speech: a whole video by its first hearing, a cut one by a second hearing before the speed-up, `verify_words` too. The report says the length, and a length that does not fit is named. `recut.py plan` moves a `pieces.json` of another rate to the rate of the finished video.
- A whole recording is a way: `takes.py trim` keeps everything between the first and the last word, and `takes.py init` makes the config from the file. `params.recording`: `takes`, `trim` or `whole`. A clean speech built from a source that is not 30 fps no longer runs at the wrong speed.
- Corrections of subtitles work on words, not on subtitle text, so they do not depend on where a subtitle breaks. `replace` is exact, `at` is one place near a second, an empty `to` takes words out, and what found no words is named. The `.srt` and `слова-таймкоды.txt` come from one list, and `run.py subtitles` puts corrections on both with no render. `run.py hear --from --to` hears a narrow piece.
- A glossary with a phrase and its first word («клод код», «клод») no longer makes «Claude код», and quotes around a word stay.
- New versions. On a real video through `final` and `sync`: ffmpeg 9.0.2 (`-filter_script` is gone, `recut.py` uses `-/filter`; loudnorm flushes a tenth of a second more, `final` holds the sound to the picture), whisper.cpp 1.9.5, Remotion 4.0.541 (licence text unchanged), React 19.3.1, TypeScript 7.0.2. By a smaller check: Playwright 1.64.1 (one captured page), HyperFrames 0.9.16 (its real render test), tesseract 5.5.4 (one picture). Node from 22.12, which Remotion's bundler needs. OpenCV stays on 4.14: version 5 gives nothing here and changes what we use. The licence line now says what it says: individuals, companies of up to three employees, non-profits.
- The file with graphics comes out in H.264 yuv420p, limited range, BT.709 with all three tags, and `final` refuses anything else. Before this it had full range or no tags, by Remotion's version, and a delivery pipeline refused it.
- A second round of markers has a way: the map always leads from the first accepted video, `recut.py old` takes a second back to it, `prepare --keep-face` keeps the face track in the scenes' seconds. `recut.py build` no longer writes through a finished video linked to it.
- The interview starts with the recording: cut or whole, speed, where the text comes from, in that order. `catalog/params.json` names who applies each parameter, `tools/aelib/coherence.py` refuses a format whose answers do not fit together, and `phases/refine.md` has «One parameter, one place».
- Brief of the scene executor: the stage is filled by the time a layout change ends, a graphic takes most of it and stands in its middle, a condition in the speech stays on screen. The sample scenes now show this too.

- A format skill says to use `python3` where there is no `python`, as the workshop already does.
- A format «Нарезка»: from a finished horizontal video, 9:16 clips with the picture whole in the centre, a headline and a call to action on the fields (`bands`), and a short teaser with a Play button, fast-forwards and a bar of the whole video's time (`teaser`). The model picks the clips by `method/clips.md`.
- A format can have paths: `variants` in `catalog/formats.json`, one row and a preset each. The workshop offers them, makes a short probe of each on the person's video (`ae.py trial --variant`), shows them side by side and builds only after a choice (`ae.py variant`); `build-skill` refuses without one. Without questions it chooses itself and says so.
- «Нарезка» has a second path, `reedit`: a re-edit for 9:16 by the overlay kit, with the preset `repurpose-reedit`, the step `reedit` and `method/reedit.md`. The pipeline agrees the clips and hands over `отрывки.json` and the words; the model makes the clips. A built skill has run on a real video as far as the clips, and the kit has made a real clip; a whole new video through a built skill has not been made yet.
- Overlay kit: `vertical.md` and the commands `vertical-init`, `vertical-check`, `vertical-words`, `vertical-final`, `vertical-sheets`. Every frame is audited for what overlaps, sits edge to edge, leaves the screen empty or leaves the safe zone. The sound comes only from clean speech (`prepare --speech`), a finished clip is measured for a hum, and the call to action stands long enough to read.
- Refining is open to any `монтаж-*`: a skill with no profile is opened as a copy and put back in place; files a profile does not describe stay (`own/`, `design/method.md`), and a `SKILL.md` edited after its build is named. `ae.py show` puts a clip the model made on the dashboard.
- A kit copied into a format now lists the hash of each file: a kit changed after its build stops the build instead of being wiped, and `ae.py kit <skill> own|renew` is the person's choice. `run.py hear` and `ae.py hear` print what the recogniser hears in a finished clip.
- `ae.py formats "<word>" --like <format>`: a narrow skill under the person's own word has the start and the paths of a format from the list. `ae.py trial --variant own:<word>` probes a way the catalog lacks beside the others.
- Two clips whose file names differ only in case or in composed letters are refused: the second would have replaced the first without a word.
- «Выпуск YouTube» no longer promises part-by-part approval, which is not built: its preset is `plan`.
- A format skill no longer claims a bare «смонтируй <путь>» when several `монтаж-*` skills are installed: the agent asks which format first.
- A 9:16 clip: `drop` takes sentences out of it, `name` names its file, `params.layout.background` colours the fields; `max_seconds` now counts the finished clip, after `speed`. The clip rubric marks down a clip that points at what a short does not have («под этим видео»).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-04

The first public version.

- «Без вопросов»: one message and a recording give the finished video and the installed skill; `phases/no-questions.md`, `ae.py init --no-questions`, `approval: auto`, `run.py edit --no-questions`. Confirmations are accepted, judgements are the agent's, a found secret is closed, every decision goes into the report.
- The `graphics` step: scenes written by the model on top of the finished video, with or without cuts; `overlay-kit/`, the `overlay` preset and a format «Наложения», `ae.py formats --preset`.
- A format skill's `SKILL.md` carries only the method it needs: `templates/format-skill/method/`.
- Overlay kit: the camera follows the face without steps (median, Gaussian, spline instead of the nearest point of the track); `time-map.json` lets a finished video take cuts by markers while scenes keep their seconds; the README holds the lessons of a 73-marker review.
- A format «YouTube с графикой»: clean speech from takes (`tools/takes.py`, a word's edge follows the sound), graphics on top, edits by markers (`tools/markers.py`, `tools/recut.py`, `takes-and-markers.md`). `phases/refine.md` says where a remark goes.
- Foundation: repository layout, one test command, CI on macOS and Windows, a monthly install check.
- Speech recognition with four providers and one word format: local whisper.cpp, ElevenLabs Scribe, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenRouter included), or your own command. Keys are read by variable name: from the environment, a `.env` file or `asr.key_file`.
- Editing mechanics on ffmpeg: file passport, speech detection, pause removal with a margin around speech in one pass, A/V drift check, loudness normalisation, `.srt` with a brand glossary.
- Format profile schema and a dependency-free profile check.
